Our Story

Your Trusted Partner in Real Estate

Daruth Properties & Real Estate is a professional real estate company dedicated to providing reliable, transparent, and quality property solutions to individuals, families, businesses, investors, and property developers.

We specialize in property sales, land transactions, housing solutions, property development, surveying, and civil engineering services. Our goal is to make property ownership and investment simple, secure, and rewarding for our clients.

At Daruth Properties, we understand that buying land or a home is an important decision. We therefore strive to provide our clients with dependable information, professional guidance, and quality property options.
